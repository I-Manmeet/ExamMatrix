    How auto-assign works

    1. Each hall needs ceil(students ÷ 30) invigilators, at least 1.
    2. After every pick, the position with the fewest eligible people is filled next.
    3. canInvigilate() checks: available that day, free in this slot, not teaching a subject in the hall, under the daily limit.
    4. From those who pass, the person with the fewest duties so far is chosen; ties are random.
    5. If nobody passes, the position is left unfilled with a reason. A rule is never broken to fill it.
